# Vision and scope

## The problem

Small businesses often end up with a website, spreadsheets, shared folders and several tools that do not quite work together. Information gets entered more than once, people cannot find the latest document, and small jobs fall between the gaps.

KM Systems aims to build practical software around those real workflows. A customer might need a better website, a staff login, or a larger management system. The starting point should fit the business, and the project should be able to grow.

## The service

### Websites that can grow

Build new business websites and maintain existing ones. Keep working platforms in place when they suit the customer. Provide a clear review, launch and handover process.

### Portals beside an existing website

A customer or staff portal can run separately at a subdomain and be linked from the existing site's navigation. It can have its own authentication, permissions, application and database.

Simple membership may be better handled by the existing website platform. Sharing a login with an existing membership system requires additional integration.

### Management systems owned by each customer

The goal is a separate working application for each customer, starting from a clean foundation and configured for their business. Possible workflows include:

- Jobs, schedules and team coordination.
- Quotes, estimates and trade-specific takeoff tools.
- Plans, documents, checklists and records.
- Timesheets, staff forms and contractor access.
- Agreed connections to storage, email, accounting and other services.

These are capabilities to scope and build for each project. A connection or an empty application project does not automatically install a finished business system.

### Setup that removes repeated admin

Use one customer setup journey to collect the relevant information, arrange access and show what is still missing. Use provider-approved app connections where practical, with manual invitations available throughout.

The customer should not have to fill out everything again when a quicker connection becomes available.

## Working principles

- Preserve the customer's existing website appearance unless a specific visual change is requested.
- Keep customer ownership, permissions and third-party costs clear.
- Keep each business's records separate.
- Review changes and test the publishing path before a production launch.
- Preserve email and other DNS-dependent services during migrations.
- Provide a practical handover and a way to recover from a bad change.
